# FreeScribe Instructions for Use (Article 13 Compliance)

## 1. System Capabilities
FreeScribe uses the Faster-Whisper model to transcribe clinical audio and generate structured medical notes locally on macOS.

## 2. Accuracy & Limitations
- **Accuracy**: Performance depends on audio quality and background noise.
- **Limitations**: The system may struggle with heavy accents or rapid technical medical jargon.
- **Hallucination**: AI can occasionally generate incorrect medical facts. Users must verify all outputs.

## 3. Output Interpretation
- **Draft Status**: All generated notes are considered 'DRAFTS'.
- **Human Oversight**: A qualified healthcare professional must review and sign off on every note before it enters a patient record.

## 4. Appropriate Use Cases
Intended for ambient clinical documentation. Not for use in emergency life-critical decision making without immediate human verification[cite: 2].
